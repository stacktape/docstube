Common objections.

?

"Won't models get so good they won't need verification?"

The opposite.

Better models write more code and more docs, so there are more unverified claims, not fewer. And a wrong API signature is wrong no matter how good the model is. Better models make verification matter more.

?

"Won't an incumbent like Mintlify just add verification?"

They can't change what they are.

Their products are built for the era where humans write the docs. AI is bolted on. Going AI-native means cannibalizing the paid, hosted business they sell. They can add a feature.

?

"Doesn't bring-your-own-compute depend on the AI vendors' terms?"

The risk works in our favor.

Worst case, the first generation costs a bit more. But saving tokens is already one of our advantages, so anything that makes tokens pricier makes that advantage bigger.

As AI spreads, verification gets more valuable, not less.
