Fail-closed canonical checkability grammar (wave 2b close, final). Existence and value evidence
(codemap grounding, the value-predicate comparison) now decide a claim's correctness ONLY when the
claim sits inside a small canonical grammar: the predicate must match a documented family
("X exists", "X is exported [from mod]", "X is defined", "X is available"; "X is|equals|defaults
to|is set to <value>"), and every word within ±5 tokens of the bound subject/value in the visible
marked prose must be on a curated neutral allowlist (plus the claim's own bound tokens), with an
explicit denial word anywhere in the marker failing outright. Prose outside the grammar is never
argued with — the claim ships honestly UNCHECKED with a journaled note, ending the negation-synonym
arms race ("`greet` is absent" can no longer ride real evidence into "checked"; unknown vocabulary
is unchecked by default). The polarity/negation fast-path repair errors stay for their better
messages; anchoring journals a non-blocking destined-unchecked phrasing hint; the writer prompt
teaches the canonical forms and tight marker spans. Documented cost: wordy-but-true positives
("`greet`, added in v2, exists") ship unchecked until the marker is tightened.
