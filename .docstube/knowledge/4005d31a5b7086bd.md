The help strings were stale and have been fixed to match the parser. Document the parser as the source of truth: wizard takes --port <n>; generate and refresh take --max-attempts <n>; refine takes [page] [--failed] [--max-pages <n>] [--max-rounds-per-page <n>] [--total-rounds <n>]; audit takes <docs-dir> [--json].
